I am a PhD Student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST).

My research focuses on computational materials science and first-principles simulations of solid-state lithium batteries. My research interests include solid electrolytes, lithium-ion transport, and electrode–electrolyte interfaces.
