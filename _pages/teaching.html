Teaching Assistant Experience

MECH2020 – Statics and Dynamics
Teaching Assistant, HKUST
Fall 2021 (2021–22); Fall 2022 (2022–23)

Fundamental course covering the equilibrium and dynamic behavior of mechanical systems. Topics include equilibrium of particles and rigid bodies, distributed forces, structural analysis of trusses, frames, cables and beams, particle and rigid-body kinematics and kinetics, Newton's second law, work and energy, momentum, and impact dynamics.
